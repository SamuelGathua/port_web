# Teller Solutions — Portfolio Website

> **Building Digital Systems That Scale**  
> An engineering-first, high-performance portfolio website built for **Teller Solutions** (Nairobi, Kenya).

---

## 🎨 Design System & Typography

The design preserves the high-contrast editorial look and feeling requested from the visual references:

1. **Editorial Typography Pairing**:
   - **Heavy Uppercase Sans**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` (800 / 900 weight) for bold, authoritative headlines.
   - **Expressive High-Contrast Italic Serif**: `Instrument Serif` (Italic) for key phrase highlights:
     - `BUILDING *digital systems* THAT SCALE.`
     - `IT IS ABOUT *engineering velocity,* NOT *cutting* CORNERS.`
     - `A ROBUST SYSTEM IS THE *starting* POINT. HOW *you scale* DEFINES YOUR FUTURE.`
     - `LET'S BUILD *something great* TOGETHER.`
   - **Developer Monospace**: `JetBrains Mono` for latency badges, code snippets, and telemetry metrics.

2. **Palette & Atmosphere**:
   - **Warm Ivory / Cream Paper Base**: `#F6F2EC`
   - **Deep Obsidian / Charcoal Black**: `#0C0D11` and `#14151C`
   - **Vibrant Primary Orange**: `#FF5500`

## 🚀 Featured Projects Showcase (Immediately following Services)

Presented in a high-contrast dark section with an alternating stacked layout and prominent primary-colored **"Visit Site"** buttons:

1. **Urim Trader**
   - **Live Site**: [urimtrader.me](https://urimtrader.me/)
   - **Description**: An AI-powered stock market simulator and learning platform for the Nairobi Securities Exchange, featuring machine learning forecasting and probability simulations.
   - **Visual**: Financial analytics terminal with live NSE tickers (`SCOM`, `EQTY`, `KCB`), Monte Carlo drift forecasting, and simulation controls.

2. **Hekimika**
   - **Live Site**: [hekimika.org](https://hekimika.org/)
   - **Description**: An innovative digital wisdom and knowledge platform designed to democratize critical research, thought leadership, and African intellectual heritage with intuitive navigation and high-performance reader architecture.
   - **Visual**: Editorial knowledge platform preview with research essays, pan-African reader metrics, and clean typography.

3. **Youth Spark Summit**
   - **Live Site**: [youthsparksummit.org](https://www.youthsparksummit.org/)
   - **Description**: The high-capacity digital portal for the Youth Spark Summit, connecting emerging African innovators, keynote sessions, registrations, and summit hackathons.
   - **Visual**: Event experience preview with keynote stages, delegate counters, and interactive session scheduling.

## 🏛️ 2-Page Architecture

### Page 1: Landing Page (`/`)
- **Sticky / Floating Navigation**: Brand logo, navigation links (`Home`, `Contact`), and prominent `"Get in Touch"` CTA button.
- **Hero Section**: Responsive typography, studio engineer portrait with geometric orange cross emblem, metric counters, and rotating circular `"SCROLL DOWN"` badge.
- **About Teller Solutions**: High-contrast dark section with verified stats and portrait.
- **Services (3-Column Grid)**: Full-Stack Development, Scalable Architecture & Cloud, Data Processing & AI.
- **Featured Projects Showcase**: Urim Trader, Hekimika, and Youth Spark Summit with live links.
- **Philosophy Banner**: Editorial typography pull-quote.
- **Footer**: Social links, Nairobi HQ, and back-to-top button.

### Page 2: Contact Page (`/contact`)
- **Left Column**: Email (`hello@tellersolutions.com`) with instant click-to-copy, Nairobi headquarters with live **EAT (UTC+3)** clock, and `<24h` SLA pledge.
- **Right Column**: Interactive project brief form with name, email, capability pills, budget selectors, message textarea (>=4 rows), full-width submit button, and celebratory confetti state.
